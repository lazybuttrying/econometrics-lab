# Communication Guidelines

## Writing style
Be clear, specific, and kind in issues and PRs.

## Constructive review norms
Critique code, not people; include rationale and alternatives.

## Assume good intent
Default to collaboration and clarification over blame.

## Helpful feedback language
- "Could we test edge case X?"
- "I suggest Y because Z."
- "Can you share repro steps for this behavior?"

## Async collaboration best practices
Share context, expected timeline, and next action in each thread.
