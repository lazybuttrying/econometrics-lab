# 6-Month Growth Plan

## Month-by-month goals
- **M1**: stabilize onboarding, close high-priority docs/issues
- **M2**: ship core probability concepts + two experiments
- **M3**: grow contributor pipeline and improve review turnaround
- **M4**: expand regression experiments and reusable helpers
- **M5**: improve interactive demos and visualization polish
- **M6**: consolidate v0.2.0 readiness and community retention

## Primary KPIs
- new contributors/month
- merged PRs/month
- issue-to-PR conversion rate
- discussion participation
- docs completion ratio

## Learning KPIs
- completed concepts
- completed experiments
- reflection completion rate
