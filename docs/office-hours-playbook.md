# Office Hours Playbook

## Purpose
Support contributors and learners with focused unblock sessions.

## Prep checklist
- Collect open questions
- Share agenda in advance
- Prepare links to relevant issues/docs

## 45-minute agenda
1. 5 min: context and goals
2. 20 min: top blockers
3. 10 min: action planning
4. 10 min: recap and ownership

## Facilitation rules
Time-box topics, rotate speakers, document decisions.

## Follow-up protocol
Post notes/actions to discussion within 24 hours.

## Artifact capture template
- Topic
- Decision
- Owner
- Due date
