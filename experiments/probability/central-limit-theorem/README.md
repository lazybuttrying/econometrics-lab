# Central Limit Theorem Experiment

## Goal
Show that sample means converge toward a normal distribution.

## Hypothesis
As sample size increases, the distribution of sample means becomes approximately normal.

## Simulation Design
Draw repeated samples from a skewed distribution and compute means.

## Parameters
Configured in `config.R`.

## Expected Result
Larger n gives tighter, more bell-shaped sampling distribution of means.
